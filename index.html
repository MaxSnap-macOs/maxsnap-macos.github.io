MaxSnap

Powerful window manager for macOS. Snap, resize, and arrange windows effortlessly. Alternative to SizeUp, Divvy, and Moom. Version 1.9.1, fully adapted to macOS on Intel and Apple Silicon (M1–M4).

Custom Shortcuts

Assign your own keyboard hotkeys for instant window control. Tailor actions to your workflow without memorizing defaults.

Precise Layouts

Choose from multiple templates to arrange windows across your screen. Halves, quarters, or custom grids—deploy in one keystroke.

Edge Snapping

Drag windows to edges or corners for automatic resizing and positioning. Natural, intuitive control over your desktop.

Multi-Monitor Ready

Manage windows seamlessly across all your displays. Trackpad gestures and menu access keep everything fluid.

Native macOS Integration

Works smoothly within macOS. No conflicts, no overhead—just efficient organization for any workflow.

How It Works

Step 1: Set Your Shortcuts

Open preferences and assign hotkeys for snap, resize, and layouts. Defaults work out of the box.

Step 2: Snap with Drag or Keys

Drag to edges/corners or trigger shortcuts. Windows resize and position automatically.

Step 3: Apply Layouts

Select a template via menu, tray icon, or gesture. All open windows arrange instantly.

Step 4: Customize Further

Tweak gestures, multi-monitor rules, and behaviors. Save presets for different setups.
